Figure-Eight Atomic Fields

Interactive, quantum-inspired geometric artwork exploring nested figure-eight fields and asynchronous three-dimensional motion.

Scientific visualization disclaimer

These animations are purely artistic and conceptual representations of atomic structure. Their figure-eight shapes, colors, rotating particle streams, and probability inspired effects are mostly stylistic choices of electron trajectories and quantum wavefunctions

Electron counts and shell populations correspond to the named elements. The animated geometry is illustrative, not physically predictive.
